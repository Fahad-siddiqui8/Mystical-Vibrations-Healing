## ✦ Key Design & Aesthetic Highlights

- **Full-Width Edge-to-Edge Architecture:** Fluid, full-bleed layouts for header, navigation, hero, and all sections spanning `100%` viewport width with generous fluid gutters (`clamp(1.5rem, 4.5vw, 5.5rem)`), eliminating narrow box constraints.
- **High-Contrast Color System:**
  - **Text Contrast:** Deep ink obsidian (`#0A0B0E`) for headings and rich charcoal (`#242730`) for body text, ensuring WCAG AAA legibility.
  - **Dynamic Contrast Anchors:** Deep obsidian announcement bar (`#0B0D10`) and footer (`#0A0B0E`) with luminous gold badges (`#E0BE60`), framing luminous alabaster (`#FAF8F5`) and warm travertine (`#ECE5D8`) sections.
  - **Crisp Card Elevation:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) floating cards with 1.5px solid burnished gold borders (`#966E20`) and warm drop shadows.
- **Editorial Luxury Typography:** Classic serif titles in *Cormorant Garamond* and *Cinzel*, paired with crisp, ultra-legible humanist sans-serif *Plus Jakarta Sans*.
- **Atmospheric Light Depth:** Frosted glassmorphism, gold hairline borders, soft luminous auric glows, and gentle spa drop shadows.
- **High-Resolution Royalty-Free Imagery:** Curated photography featuring quartz crystal singing bowls, bronze Tibetan singing bowls, planetary gongs, amber apothecary botanicals, and candlelit meditation sanctuaries.
